Do You Recognize These Problems?

Feed is the biggest running cost of any gamefowl yard. Yet most breeders still mix “by eye”, copy a recipe from a friend or buy bagged feed without knowing what is inside. The result is familiar: chicks that grow uneven, stags that never fill out, hens that stop laying or give poor fertility, grain that goes moldy in the bin and a cost per bag that keeps climbing. The problem is rarely the bloodline. It is the lack of a method: know the requirements, know the ingredients, formulate, mix correctly, control quality and calculate costs, batch after batch.
