Disease Guide

When to Call for Help

🚨 Call now — vet, state veterinarian or USDA 1-866-536-7593

  • Several birds sick or dying within a day or two
  • Purple, swollen combs and wattles
  • Twisted necks, tremors or paralysis with breathing trouble
  • Sudden crash in laying with coughing or gasping

Bird flu and virulent Newcastle disease must be reported. Don’t move birds, eggs or equipment off the property until you’ve spoken to someone.

📞 Call a vet today

  • Egg binding or prolapse
  • Open-mouth breathing at rest
  • Bloody droppings in chicks
  • Bird can’t stand or won’t drink
  • Heat stress that doesn’t improve in shade

👀 Watch and care

  • Mild sneeze in one bird, still eating
  • Feather loss in fall (molt)
  • Small foot scab without swelling
  • Mites or lice (treat flock and coop)

🛡️ Biosecurity basics

  • Quarantine new or show birds 30 days
  • Dedicated coop boots and clothes
  • Keep wild birds away from feed and water
  • Buy from NPIP-tested flocks

ℹ️ About this app

  • Educational guidance, not a diagnosis
  • No medication doses — your vet decides treatment and withdrawal times
  • Photos are sent to Claude for the AI check; results are saved only on this device
