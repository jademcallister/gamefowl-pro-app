What is it?

The Gamefowl Pro Online Conformation Show is a online show open to breeders in the United States and internationally. It is built for beginners and experienced breeders alike: show your birds in a competitive arena, meet other breeders and learn more about breeding toward sound conformation together. We run youth divisions (open to youth exhibitors) and open divisions for everyone.

Entries are photos of your birds, judged live on video. While it is not the same as an in-person show, entries have specific photo requirements to make it as close to the real thing as possible. Exhibitors and spectators can watch placings happen in real time, ask the judge questions and hear exactly why each bird placed where it did.

The goal is to promote sound breeding, education and excellence in a way that is open to everyone. Thank you to everyone who enters, shares, sponsors or helps in any way.

Entries

Each entry is a set of photos of one bird. The bird should stand as naturally as possible, on a plain background, with good light. Comb, eyes, beak, both feet and the tail must be visible.

1. Side profileRequired · standing naturally
2. Front viewLeg set, keel, wing evenness
3. Rear viewHocks, tail set, twist
4. Feet close-upToes, claws and pads
5. Walking videoOptional · gait (link)

Show rules

  • This is an exhibition show of conformation and soundness only.
  • Photos showing sparring, attached gaffs or spurs, wounds or any fighting will be disqualified.
  • Birds must be owned by the exhibitor. One entry per bird; photos must be recent and unedited.
  • Youth exhibitors must be 18 or under on the entry deadline.
  • The judge’s decision is final. Areas the photos don’t show are marked “not checked”, never guessed.

    How Birds Are Judged

    Every bird is scored 1–10 on the same 8 checkpoints, in this order. The overall score is the average of the areas the photos actually show. Soundness problems (sores, swollen joints, limping, a twisted keel) are flagged separately from the score.

    1. Toes & claws straight, fully formed, clean pads
    2. Feet & shanks even support, no swelling or twisting
    3. Hocks & thighs even joints, proportionate thighs
    4. Stance & gait stable stance, even steps (video)
    5. Body & keel balanced, straight back, straight keel
    6. Wings & shoulders carried evenly, no droop
    7. Neck, head & crest moves freely, crest clear of eyes
    8. Tail & tail set balanced and carried naturally

    🧮 Try the judge’s scorecard

    Score your own bird the way the judge will. Leave an area on “not checked” if you can’t see it.

    🤖 AI Judging Panel

    Three AI judges score your bird’s photos on the same 8 checkpoints, each with a different focus. The panel takes the middle score on every checkpoint, flags soundness concerns, and a head judge writes the comments. AI scores are advisory — a human judge confirms every placing and reviews any flag.

    🦶 Soundness JudgeToes, feet, hocks, stance
    ⚖️ Type & Balance JudgeBody, keel, wings, tail set
    👁️ Breeder’s Eye JudgeWhole bird, neck, head, crest

    * Side profile required. Plain background, good light, bird standing naturally. More views = fewer “not checked”.
